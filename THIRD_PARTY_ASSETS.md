# Third-party assets

Neon Cube uses redistributable game assets from the following creators.

## Kenney — Blaster Kit 2.1
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0)
- Included: selected GLB weapon models and shared colormap texture.
- Original license: `assets/third_party/kenney_blaster/License.txt`.

## Quaternius — Cyberpunk Game Kit
- Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/cyberpunkgamekit.html
- License on the Cyberpunk Game Kit release: CC0
- Included: selected enemy models and environment props.
- Original archive license: `assets/third_party/quaternius_cyberpunk/License.txt`.

The assets are integrated into Neon Cube and are not redistributed as a standalone asset pack.
