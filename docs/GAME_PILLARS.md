# Neon Cube — Game Pillars

## 1. Six-face mastery
The world is not a gimmick room. Gravity transitions, traversal routes, encounter design, sightlines and enemy tactics must exploit the cube geometry continuously.

Player expectation:
- I can read which face I am on,
- I can intentionally transition to another face,
- I can use gravity transitions tactically,
- camera/input never feel broken by the mechanic.

## 2. Aggressive cyberpunk gunplay
Combat should be fast, readable and physical:
- distinct recoil,
- strong muzzle/impact feedback,
- enemy hit reactions,
- movement that supports aggression,
- clear threat silhouettes,
- weapons with roles rather than numeric reskins.

## 3. Dense authored city
Each face is a district, not a palette swap.
The six faces require:
- unique skyline language,
- street furniture,
- signage,
- materials,
- lighting identity,
- traversal motif,
- encounter motif,
- landmark visible from multiple faces.

## 4. Tactical enemies
Enemy types must differ in decision-making:
- flanking,
- suppression,
- pursuit across edges,
- distance management,
- area denial,
- coordinated pressure,
- boss phase behavior.

## 5. Premium presentation
No debug/prototype feel in the shipping path:
- animation,
- VFX,
- sound,
- UI,
- transitions,
- loading,
- menus,
- fail/victory states,
- cinematics all share a coherent art direction.

## Non-goals
- procedurally generating everything just to increase content count,
- adding dozens of weapons/enemies that only differ in stats,
- hiding weak level design behind neon bloom,
- calling automated-test success equivalent to game quality.
